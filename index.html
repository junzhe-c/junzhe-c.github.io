About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Tianjin University. Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University (January 2024–May 2025) and my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University (2019–2023).

My research focuses on computer vision, video understanding, and vision-language models. I am particularly interested in how models preserve object identity and state over time, reason from visual evidence, and represent videos efficiently. My earlier work includes tiny-object detection and mixed-precision quantization.

I welcome discussions about research and potential collaborations. Please feel free to contact me.
